Data: NASA FIRMS (serves both MODIS, since 2000, and VIIRS, since ~2012, active-fire detections). The gap: VIIRS's finer ~375m resolution reliably detects more, smaller fires than MODIS's ~1km resolution — the same event, two different counts. Baseline: a 10-year seasonal average for this region and time of year; "anomaly" means statistically unusual against that average, not a claim about cause. ML cross-check: the Isolation Forest score agrees with the rule-based tier on every day in this set except two low-activity days it also flags as mildly unusual (real models don't always match a hand-written rule exactly — that's expected, not hidden) — run it yourself from ml/anomaly_model.py in the repo. On a dataset this small (30 days) it's a proof of methodology, not a production-trained model.

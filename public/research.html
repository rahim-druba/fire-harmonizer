What the literature tells us

The main findings behind Fire Harmonizer. Published results are shown in their original regional and sensor context—not presented as measurements from our own model.

014.8×

Vadrevu & Lasko · 2018 ↗

More detections in Punjab

VIIRS detected 4.8 times as many fires as Terra and Aqua MODIS combined in the study’s agricultural landscape. Compared with Aqua alone, the factor was 6.5.

For this project: A regional ratio explains why a gap can be large; it is not a calibration factor for Kazakhstan.

025.13×

White · 2022 ↗

The ratio changes with location

Across South America in 2012–2021, Suomi NPP VIIRS detected 5.13 times as many fire pixels as Aqua MODIS. Country ratios ranged from 3.87 in Chile to 6.81 in Venezuela.

For this project: We keep both sensors visible rather than multiplying MODIS by one universal number.

03Scale matters

Li et al. · 2018 ↗

Fire radiative power is not a pixel count

MODIS and VIIRS fire radiative power (FRP) was generally comparable in fire clusters, but the relationship changed with viewing angle, region and aggregation scale. Large boreal fires were a notable exception.

For this project: A result about radiative power cannot be applied directly to daily hotspot counts.

0442.8%

Li et al. · 2020 ↗

Missed low-intensity fires affect the total

In the African study, missing low-intensity fires and swath gaps contributed to MODIS daily FRP being underestimated by at least 42.8% relative to VIIRS.

For this project: A lower sensor total does not automatically mean less burning on the ground.

05Partial overlap

Fu et al. · 2020 ↗

Neither product is a complete reference

A Northeast Asia comparison found that only some MODIS and VIIRS fire detections were co-located, with different matching rates in forests and low-biomass land.

For this project: We treat sensor agreement as a comparison, not as validation against ground truth.

06Correct, then combine

Zheng et al. · 2026 ↗

Harmonization requires more than addition

The study applies duplicate correction, near-synchronous matching, cloud correction and view-angle-dependent FRP adjustment before fusing Aqua MODIS and Suomi NPP VIIRS observations.

For this project: Full calibration and deduplication remain future work for Fire Harmonizer.

07Different product

Giglio et al. · 2025 ↗

Burned area and active fire answer different questions

The VIIRS burned-area product and MODIS burned-area product showed spatial and temporal consistency with similar accuracy in global validation. This concerns burned-area mapping, not equality of hotspot counts.

For this project: Our calendar reports active-fire detections; it does not estimate hectares burned.

Our deliberate scope

We compare the sensors, use the 75-day median as a transparent proxy baseline, and cross-check unusual days with Isolation Forest. We do not claim full sensor calibration, a multi-year seasonal climatology or ground-truth accuracy.

Based on the project’s research notes, checked against the linked papers. White’s paper is dated 2022 in the journal, although the earlier notes list 2023.

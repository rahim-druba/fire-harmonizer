From orbit to a fire record

MODIS and VIIRS are two types of satellite instrument. NASA FIRMS turns their thermal observations into the hotspot records used here.

Why the two counts differ

Pixel size, sensitivity, viewing angle, cloud cover and overpass timing affect what each instrument detects. A hotspot is a detection within a satellite pixel, not a mapped fire perimeter or a count of unique fires. Smaller fires can occupy only part of a pixel.

How the observations reach this app

  1. Satellite instrumentsThermal measurements from MODIS and VIIRS
  2. NASA FIRMSActive-fire records with location, time and sensor attributes
  3. Fire HarmonizerDaily sensor counts, a median baseline and an offline ML cross-check
  4. Your viewA historical calendar, plus separate live region lookups

The calendar contains 75 days of Kazakhstan-wide observations, from 1 September to 14 November 2026. The region picker requests fresh FIRMS counts for the area and dates you choose. These two views are not yet connected.
